Member Compact

We come together to enrich what we are working on and to shape our thinking. This compact is how we protect this precious space.

  1. The club is only as good as what each member puts into it

    Membership is not spectatorship. We are here to contribute, not to observe. That means showing up, sharing something unfinished, articulating unexpected ideas and forming half-formed questions.

  2. We are a trusted intimate group, not a quotable source

    You may share your own outputs freely in public contexts. When the club shapes your thinking, acknowledge the space. Do not reference what others said or attribute their ideas without explicit permission.

  3. We disagree with curiosity, not dismissal

    Multiple perspectives on AI set the condition of the club. Skeptics and enthusiasts sit at the same table. What we owe each other is genuine positive engagement.

  4. This compact is valid even after you are no longer a club member
